Finch Email Setup — 2 Minutes
===============================

You need a Gmail address. That's it. Here's the fastest way:

Option A: Create a dedicated Gmail for Finch (recommended)
  1. Go to gmail.com → Create account
  2. Pick a name like "finch.yourcompany@gmail.com"
  3. This keeps your personal inbox clean, prospects see a pro address

Option B: Use your existing Gmail
  Already have one? Use it. No new account needed.

Then — ONE STEP:
  1. Go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
  2. Select "Mail" → "Other" → name it "Finch"
  3. Copy the 16-character password Google gives you

Now run these two commands:
  export FINCH_EMAIL="your-email@gmail.com"
  export FINCH_EMAIL_PASSWORD="the-16-char-app-password"

Then restart Finch:
  cd /home/user/finch && python3 web/chat_server.py

That's it. Finch now:
  - Sends cold outreach emails from your address
  - Monitors your inbox for replies
  - Responds autonomously to prospects who reply

NO credit card. NO API keys. NO third-party services.
Just your Gmail and a 16-character app password.
