Questions, answered plainly.

Does Aegis test or exploit our systems?

No. Aegis is limited to passive public-information monitoring of assets you approve. It does not authenticate, submit forms, exploit vulnerabilities or access protected information.

Does an alert mean we have been breached?

No. Reports label observations and potential indicators carefully. A change or advisory match requires verification by your IT team and is not evidence of exploitation or compromise.

Can we remove an asset?

Yes. Customers control the approved list. Removing an asset stops future monitoring of it.

Do I need a sales call?

No. The service, limitations and fixed founding price are explained here. Harold can answer product questions by text, and the customer journey is designed for self-service.
