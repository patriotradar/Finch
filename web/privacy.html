Privacy notice

Version 1.0 · pre-launch review copy

Aegis is software operated by Jamie Mahon, a UK sole trader. Final privacy contact details must be confirmed before production launch.

Information processed

  • Account and company details supplied by customers.
  • Customer-approved internet assets and authority confirmations.
  • Public observations, evidence, reports and monitoring request logs.
  • Payment references and licence status; full payment-card details are not stored by Aegis.
  • Support conversations, outreach sources, replies and opt-out records.

Purposes

Information is used to provide the service, secure accounts, maintain customer-controlled scope, generate reports, support customers, administer licences and honour suppression requests.

Retention and rights

Customers may request access, correction, export or deletion subject to legal and security-record obligations. Permanent suppression records are retained so an opt-out is not accidentally reversed. Production retention periods, subprocessors, lawful bases, international-transfer details and the final privacy contact must be legally reviewed before launch.

Security

Passwords are hashed, private routes require authentication, customer records are workspace-scoped, and deployment secrets are stored as environment variables.
