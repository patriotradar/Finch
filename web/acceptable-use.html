Acceptable-use policy

Version 1.0 · pre-launch review copy

Authorised assets only

Customers may add only assets they own or have explicit authority to monitor. Aegis records the confirming user and date. Removing an asset stops future monitoring.

Prohibited use

  • Monitoring third parties without authority.
  • Using observations to exploit, intimidate or misrepresent risk.
  • Attempting authentication, form submission, directory brute force, account guessing or protected-data access through Aegis.
  • Presenting a potential indicator as proof of compromise.

Monitoring boundaries

Aegis uses public certificate-transparency, DNS, TLS, email-security, root HTTP-header and published-advisory information with conservative limits. It stops on instability or rate limiting.

Enforcement

Access may be suspended where authorisation is doubtful, policy is breached or safe operation cannot be maintained.
