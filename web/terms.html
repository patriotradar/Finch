Subscription terms

Version 1.0 · pre-launch review copy

The founding Aegis licence is £995 for 12 months and includes up to five authorised users and 25 customer-approved assets. There are no hidden monthly charges.

Service

Aegis provides passive public-information monitoring and evidence-led reporting. It is not penetration testing, managed security, incident response, insurance, legal advice or a guarantee that vulnerabilities or incidents will be found.

Customer responsibilities

The customer must approve only assets it owns or is authorised to manage, maintain accurate account information and arrange qualified IT verification before acting on potential indicators.

Renewal, cancellation and refunds

The renewal date and payment status are shown in the customer account. Cancellation prevents renewal but does not erase records that must be retained. Final renewal notice periods, refund eligibility and statutory rights require legal confirmation before checkout is enabled.

Availability and liability

Monitoring may be limited by public-source availability, rate limiting, instability and incomplete evidence. Final liability wording and governing-law provisions must be professionally reviewed before launch.
